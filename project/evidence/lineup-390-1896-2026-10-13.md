# Lineup editor at 390px: live evidence for #1896

An isolated lane backend from this worktree (`make dev-be`, its own SQLite), the demo library
(`make demo-library` + `make demo-seed`, invented titles), and the Vite frontend (`make dev-fe`).
The filler jobs and channel maintenance were paused first. Chromium at 390×844 (2× device
scale), signed in as the lane's dev admin, on Retro Rewind's Programming tab (five series rows).

At a 390px viewport the lineup list is **218px** wide: the app rail plus the page, block and
section padding leave the editor that much.
