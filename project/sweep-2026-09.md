## TL;DR

1. **The verdict holds for the core and fails for the repo as a whole.** The core (142k lines,
   excluding filler and tooling) is still layered, gated and nearly free of TODO debt. But
   filler is now 107k lines (38% of all Go), and it reaches into the core: 16 filler packages
   (56k lines, 28% of the server-linked Go) are compiled into the server, including
   bake-off, eval and research code. `internal/store` imports 8 filler packages and holds 21
   filler files.
2. **One real defect found: two cron dialects.** Settings validate schedules with `gronx`, and
   River runs them with `robfig/cron`. Six expressions that the settings page accepts are rejected
   by River. On a live lane backend, one saved `L` schedule kept River from starting at all: every
   scheduled job stopped while healthz stayed green, and the log blamed a code default. Fixed in
   #1731.
3. **The cheapest CI win is a test fixture.** `internal/reconcile` takes 430 s in race lane 1/2
   (2.3 s without `-race`), because each test migrates a fresh SQLite database. The
   migrate-once template from #1578 already exists, and at least four slow packages don't use it.
4. **Reliability, not speed, is now the merge queue's problem.** The median queue run is ~10 min
   (down from 19 in #1570's baseline). But 10 of the last 40 runs failed, and 6 of those 10
   involved the two known tuner-surf flakes (#1443, #1492). Quarantine (#1570 step 3) isn't
   built yet.
5. **Most of the rest is healthy and measured, not guessed.** Duplication is low, knip is clean,
   Make targets are all referenced, there are 28 direct Go dependencies, and there are 3 TODOs.
   See [Measured and left alone](#measured-and-left-alone).

## How it was measured

- **Dead code:** `deadcode ./cmd/...` (golang.org/x/tools, latest), then `git grep -w` per
  function to split production references from test references and to check for build tags.
- **Import graph:** `go list -f '{{.ImportPath}} {{join .Imports " "}}' ./internal/... ./cmd/...`
  for fan-in and fan-out, and `go list -deps ./cmd/loomarr` for what the server links.
- **Size:** `wc -l` over non-test `.go` files per package, and over `web/apps` and
  `web/packages` `.ts`/`.tsx` files excluding tests, stories and generated code.
- **Complexity:** `gocyclo -over 30 internal cmd` (fzipp/gocyclo), excluding tests.
- **Duplication:** `dupl -t 120` over non-test Go, and `jscpd@4 --min-tokens 100` over web.
- **CI:** the job durations of the last 20 green `merge_group` runs of `ci.yml`
  (`gh run view --json jobs`), and the failed jobs of the last 40 runs. Package times come from the
  race-lane log of run 36418991198.
- **Cron repro:** a scratch module pinned to `gronx v1.20.3` and `robfig/cron/v3 v3.0.1`, calling
  both parsers with the same six-field setup `internal/scheduler/river.go` uses.
