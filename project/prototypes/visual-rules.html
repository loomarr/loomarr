1 · Accent roles: amber means focus or the primary action, nothing else

The amber exists once in the tokens (#FFB020). It is brand.signal, action.primary and action.focus (design-system/src/tokens/tokens.ts:44-46,152), and on web it is also --primary and --ring (apps/web/src/styles.css). Today it carries six meanings: focus, primary action, selection, identity text, status and warning. The proposal keeps two: focus (a ring or border) and the primary action (a solid fill, at most one per view). It also keeps the brand moments (the colour bars, the tuner lock and the wordmark), which never sit next to an interactive state. This is the native board's own header ("signal for focus and the primary action only", redesign map § Native) and the TV 5b rule ("focus is the only amber").

The desktop rail and NavTabs draw "you are here" in amber. The phone bottom bar (the same destinations, #1849) already draws it neutral and keeps amber for focus. So the same "current page" state is amber on desktop and grey on a phone.
