> **Beta scope:** This release is about stability. It fixes the filler pipeline stalls and livelocks, the
> daily database lockup, guide latency and blanking, LLM timeouts on single-slot local servers, the
> unauthenticated metrics endpoint, and the playout boundary and recovery defects found in sustained
> household use. Queue becomes a Requests page.
>
> It also makes two journeys fast. **Add a channel** now streams its picks while the model generates
> them. It finishes in roughly 20–30 s on a local 2-slot server, where it used to take 42–75 s, and
> titles named in the request ("like The Goonies") are grounded directly. **Channel switching** uses
> prepared media, pre-warms neighbouring channels, and shows the channel's latest still the moment you
> switch: 0.3–0.5 s on the Web and about 0.5–1.8 s on the Android TV emulator.
>
> **Known limitations:**
> - The TV switch timings were measured on an x86_64 emulator, not on a Shield, so treat them as shape
>   rather than device numbers. Certifying surfing across 100+ channels is beta.8 work (#1037, #1460).
> - Generation prompt v16 is verified on the live library but not yet certified by a pre-release
>   quality gate (#1015, beta.8).
> - On NVENC, prepared media's storage estimate is conservative (#1482).
> - An intermittent WebKit end-to-end flake remains under investigation (#1443).
> - Two Android TV reference screenshots (pairing, watching-loading) predate this release's UI.
