> **Release candidate.** This is the sixth candidate for beta.8, published so the real-Shield surf
> and the 24 h soak can run against it on a household install. It is a prerelease: `latest` stays on
> beta.7, and the final `v0.2.0-beta.8` tag follows once the gates below are verified live.
>
> **Changes since rc.5:**
> - **A tune isn't refused while idle work holds capacity (#1780).** On a real Shield, about one
>   channel change in three got a 503 because speculative neighbour warms, sessions in their grace
>   period and unfetched 4K premiums held the playout budget. A viewer's tune now evicts that idle
>   work first, and is refused only when real viewers fill the budget.
> - **The TV's channel-switch readout appears with the key (#1781).** The "CH n / TUNING IN" readout
>   was built after the key arrived, about 300 ms late on a Shield. It is now built in advance, so the
>   key only shows it. The TV switch also reads as the B4 design: snow, locked bars, the channel
>   number and TUNING IN (#1627).
> - **Watched channels stop re-airing yesterday (#1694).** On large film channels with a regular
>   viewer, next-day repeats rose after the window boundary moved to local midnight. The window now
>   tiles a steady deck, so repeats return to their earlier rate.
> - **Compilation reels wait for certified splitting (#1773).** Long files made of several clips are
>   no longer split automatically; they wait behind `filler.acquisition.compilations` (off by
>   default) until splitting is certified. Clips from a filler folder are unaffected.
> - **Decision timestamps repaired (#1760).** Filler review decisions were stamped in nanoseconds
>   where seconds were expected; migration 00131 repairs the stored rows.
> - **A paired TV has member access (#1772).** A TV paired by an admin no longer acts as an admin.
>   Pairing now says so.
> - **Requests (#1659).** Times read relative to now, and bulk Approve stays disabled until something
>   is selected.
> - **Release evidence (#661).** Every release runs the ffmpeg test suite against the ffmpeg it ships,
>   and publishes only images carrying an SBOM and provenance for the tagged commit.
> - **Cleanup (#1569, #1747).** More dead filler code is deleted, and filler's enrichment, research
>   and decision storage move behind their own package. Behaviour is unchanged.
>
> **Earlier candidates:** filler clips publish again, schedules, the prepared-media cleanup and the
> first redesigned web screens (rc.5); black 4K HDR letterbox bars on Intel GPUs, watching no longer
> rewriting what's on air, filler supply and settings that apply live (rc.4); the OpenCL watermark,
> the analog channel switch and compound genres (rc.3); the watermark speed check and
> keyframe-indexed tune-in (rc.2).
>
> **Beta scope: on-demand playout (#1512).** Channels are no longer encoded ahead of time. Prepared
> media is gone: no encoder runs while nobody is watching, and a tune starts one transcode for that
> channel. A long-lived channel packager owns each channel's timeline. It serves 1 s fMP4 HLS to the
> Web and TV apps and continuous MPEG-TS to media-server tuners from the same encode, with no format
> change at programme ↔ commercial boundaries (G1, G4).
>
> - **Resource-aware admission (G5).** A ResourceBudget sizes playout from measured capacity (GPU,
>   encoder sessions and the container's CPU allowance) and prices each stream by its source class.
>   On slow CPU-only hosts a software ladder steps quality down by measured speed instead of refusing.
>   Filler work is capped and yields to playback.
> - **Uniform output (G6, G7).** Every channel airs H.264 with AAC stereo in one fixed geometry, from
>   media files read directly. Stream facts, keyframe index and loudness come from Loomarr's own
>   measurements, never from the media server at airtime. Live loudnorm is replaced by static gain.
> - **Commercial breaks** air mid-programme at measured scene fades, on by default, with a
>   per-channel off switch.
> - **4K (G10).** Channels with meaningful 4K content offer a premium HEVC format, 4K SDR or 4K
>   HDR10, alongside the 1080p SDR baseline. The master playlist lists it, and only clients that can
>   decode it take it. Every other client keeps the baseline.
> - **Tone mapping (G11).** HDR sources are tone-mapped on the GPU with an install-wide curve
>   (`playout.tone_curve`, Hable by default). The image ships the GPU tone-map runtime, and a
>   startup self-check fails loudly if the picture comes out black.
> - **Surfing (G3).** Neighbouring channels are warmed speculatively, and a cold channel shows its
>   latest still until the first frame arrives.
> - **ffmpeg 9.** The image ships FFmpeg n9.0.1 (BtbN `n9.0.1-11-ge47273f4d9`). Its corresponding
>   source is published in
>   [third-party-sources-2026-09-27](https://github.com/loomarr/loomarr/releases/tag/third-party-sources-2026-09-27);
>   the other third-party sources are unchanged from
>   [third-party-sources-2026-08-31](https://github.com/loomarr/loomarr/releases/tag/third-party-sources-2026-08-31).
>
> **Known limitations:**
> - The real-Shield surf (G3) and the 24 h soak (G4: fewer than 1 stall per viewer-hour) run against
>   this candidate (#1037). rc.5's Shield surf measured warm switches slower than the 600 ms p95
>   target; this candidate removes the refusals behind part of that, and the re-measure decides the
>   rest.
> - The web app has no phone layout yet: at phone width it shows the desktop layout (#1785).
> - The redesigned screens still use placeholders where a design is pending: missing artwork, the
>   Guide's channel menu contents, and the LLM-off describe panel.
