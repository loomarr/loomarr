> **Release candidate.** This is the fifth candidate for beta.8, published so it can be deployed and
> measured live on a household install. It is a prerelease: `latest` stays on beta.7, and the final
> `v0.2.0-beta.8` tag follows once the gates below are verified live.
>
> **Changes since rc.4:**
> - **Filler clips publish again (#1540, #1719).** The conditioning check measured clip ends from time
>   zero, but encoded clips start about 45 ms in (AAC priming), so every re-encoded filler clip was
>   rejected. Clips with a detected silence, black or freeze interval near the end were rejected too.
>   Quality evidence now uses each file's real timeline, and a conditioned clip keeps its own
>   measurement. The checks are not loosened.
> - **Schedules the settings page accepts now run (#1724).** Settings validated schedules with one
>   cron dialect and the job runner parsed them with another, so some valid schedules stopped boot or
>   never ran. Both now use the same dialect.
> - **The retired prepared-media library is removed on first boot (#1563).** Only what the old
>   library wrote is removed, in the background, and the space reclaimed is logged.
> - **One playout admission count (#1562).** The encode pool's gate is folded into the resource
>   ledger, which fixes a case where a stream the ledger admitted was still refused.
> - **The new web design, first screens (#1659).** The Guide on a shared grid (keyboard navigation,
>   jump to channel, a 2/4/6/12-hour span picker; paused channels in a neutral tone), Home (status,
>   Watching now, Tonight, and channel ideas for members), Watch (a channels drawer and 0–9 direct
>   tune), Channel detail, and Filler (coverage cards and a Manage hub). People shows when each person
>   was last seen. Control borders now meet 3:1 contrast everywhere.
> - **Channel ideas without the LLM (#1720).** Each idea gets a name and a one-line pitch built from
>   the library and the seasonal calendar, and members can request one.
> - **Cleanup (#1569, #1718).** The retired encoder builders and dead filler code are deleted, the
>   filler research tooling moves to its own `research/` module outside the server, and filler's
>   store files move behind their own package. Behaviour is unchanged.
>
> **Changes in rc.4:** black 4K HDR letterbox bars on Intel GPUs (#1673); watching no longer rewrites
> what's on air (#1674); no daily mid-episode cut at the window boundary (#1675); channels air more of
> their filler (#1684); filler supply (#1452, #749, #1420, #1488); settings that apply live.
>
> **Earlier candidates:** the OpenCL watermark and its settings, the analog channel switch and
> compound genres (rc.3); the watermark speed check and keyframe-indexed tune-in (rc.2).
>
> **Beta scope: on-demand playout (#1512).** Channels are no longer encoded ahead of time. Prepared
> media is gone: no encoder runs while nobody is watching, and a tune starts one transcode for that
> channel. A long-lived channel packager owns each channel's timeline. It serves 1 s fMP4 HLS to the
> Web and TV apps and continuous MPEG-TS to media-server tuners from the same encode, with no format
> change at programme ↔ commercial boundaries (G1, G4).
>
> - **Resource-aware admission (G5).** A ResourceBudget sizes playout from measured capacity (GPU,
>   encoder sessions and the container's CPU allowance) and prices each stream by its source class.
>   On slow CPU-only hosts a software ladder steps quality down by measured speed instead of refusing.
>   Filler work is capped and yields to playback.
> - **Uniform output (G6, G7).** Every channel airs H.264 with AAC stereo in one fixed geometry, from
>   media files read directly. Stream facts, keyframe index and loudness come from Loomarr's own
>   measurements, never from the media server at airtime. Live loudnorm is replaced by static gain.
> - **Commercial breaks** air mid-programme at measured scene fades, on by default, with a
>   per-channel off switch.
> - **4K (G10).** Channels with meaningful 4K content offer a premium HEVC format, 4K SDR or 4K
>   HDR10, alongside the 1080p SDR baseline. The master playlist lists it, and only clients that can
>   decode it take it. Every other client keeps the baseline.
> - **Tone mapping (G11).** HDR sources are tone-mapped on the GPU with an install-wide curve
>   (`playout.tone_curve`, Hable by default). The image ships the GPU tone-map runtime, and a
>   startup self-check fails loudly if the picture comes out black.
> - **Surfing (G3).** Neighbouring channels are warmed speculatively, and a cold channel shows its
>   latest still until the first frame arrives.
> - **ffmpeg 9.** The image ships FFmpeg n9.0.1 (BtbN `n9.0.1-11-ge47273f4d9`). Its corresponding
>   source is published in
>   [third-party-sources-2026-09-27](https://github.com/loomarr/loomarr/releases/tag/third-party-sources-2026-09-27);
>   the other third-party sources are unchanged from
>   [third-party-sources-2026-08-31](https://github.com/loomarr/loomarr/releases/tag/third-party-sources-2026-08-31).
>
> **Known limitations:**
> - The 24 h soak (G4: fewer than 1 stall per viewer-hour) has not run yet (#1037). Tuning,
>   surfing and 4K HDR playback have been checked by hand on a real Shield.
> - On large film channels with a regular viewer, next-day repeats rose after the window boundary
>   moved to local midnight (#1694, under investigation before the final tag).
> - The redesigned screens still use placeholders where a design is pending: missing artwork, the
>   Guide's channel menu contents, and the LLM-off describe panel.
